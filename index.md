# AI-DLC Adoption Bootcamp

**Get productive on AWS AI-DLC (v2) in under a week.** A role-based, hands-on program for **Developers, Product Managers, and UX Designers** — with a strong shared core everyone takes, thin role add-ons, and a team capstone.

> New here? Read **[C1 — Why AI-DLC](modules/shared-core/C1-why-aidlc.md)** first, then work straight down the Shared Core.

## How the bootcamp works

- **Shared Core (all roles)** builds the common foundation — the method, the tooling, and the habits.
- **Role add-ons** go deep where you add the most value (pick one, or take all if you're "ambidextrous").
- **Capstone** runs the whole method as a mixed-role team on a real `workshop`-scope project.
- Every lesson follows a **read → see → do → check** rhythm, with a **terminology toggle** (AI-DLC term ↔ the traditional Agile analogue) and a short self-check quiz.

Two ways to pace it: **five half-days**, or a **2.5-day dedicated block** — see the [full program plan](aidlc-bootcamp-plan.md).
